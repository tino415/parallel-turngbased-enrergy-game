Prototype rules

Nodes start with active and invested energy. Invested principal is locked and earns 10% in active energy. Active energy decays 10% each round. Each node naturally generates 1 active energy. Energy transfers only across one edge. Each node may invest once and allocate energy to any number of neighboring nodes, retaining unallocated energy for defense. Attacks are grouped by destination and attacker, then shuffled; attack power uses an independent ±20% modifier for each combatant. Captured nodes lose a random 25–75% of investment. Policies persist: reserve energy, invest surplus, and transfer to an adjacent friend. Manual allocations take priority. Victory is achieved by eliminating the opposing player. Neutral nodes do not have to be conquered.
