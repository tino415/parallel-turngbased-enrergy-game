Prototype rules

Nodes start with active and invested energy. Each new match has 250 sustainable total energy per owned node (active + invested); additional energy is allowed, but 25% of the excess decays after production and normal active decay each round, proportionally from active and invested. Withdraw invested energy into immediately spendable active energy with a 5% fee. Remaining invested principal earns 10% as active income each turn. Active energy decays 10% and each node produces +1. Energy transfers only across one edge. Each node may invest once and allocate energy to any number of neighboring nodes, retaining unallocated energy for defense. Attacks are grouped by destination and attacker, then shuffled; attack power uses an independent ±20% modifier for each combatant. Captured nodes lose a random 25–75% of investment. Policies persist: reserve energy, invest surplus, and transfer to an adjacent friend. Manual allocations take priority. Victory is achieved by eliminating the opposing player. Neutral nodes do not have to be conquered.
